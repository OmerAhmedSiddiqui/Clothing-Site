# Outreach messages — Houston boutiques

Before sending, replace:
- `[Name]` — your first name
- `[Studio]` — your business name
- `[Demo link]` — the live Maison Ardent link after you deploy
- `[Owner]` — the owner's first name if their profile shows it, otherwise use "Hi there"

Tips:
- Check each store's Instagram bio first. If it already links to a website, skip that store.
- Send DMs from your business Instagram account, not a personal one.
- One follow-up after 3–4 days is fine. If there's no reply after that, stop.
- Don't paste the same text to everyone. Each message below mentions something specific about that store.

## Email template (when you find an email address)

US anti-spam law (CAN-SPAM) requires a truthful subject line, your real physical business address, and an easy way to opt out. The bottom three lines of this template cover the address and opt-out, so keep them.

**Subject:** A website idea for [Store name]

> Hi [Owner],
>
> I'm [Name], a web designer in Houston. I came across [Store name] on Google Maps. [One specific compliment, e.g. "your 4.9 rating from 160 reviews"]. I noticed you don't have a website yet.
>
> I build scroll-story websites for clothing boutiques. Here's a sample: [Demo link]
>
> I'd be glad to make a free homepage mockup for [Store name], with no obligation. Just reply "yes" and I'll send it within a few days.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]
> [Studio] · [Phone]
> [Your business mailing address]
>
> *Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.*
